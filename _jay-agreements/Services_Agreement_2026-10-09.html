SERVICES AGREEMENT

Client Dashboard App and Online Listings for Nutrition Intuition

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of October 9, 2026 between Jay Cavada ("Developer") and Nutrition Intuition, LLC, an Arizona limited liability company, represented by Amber Barcellos ("Client"). Together, the "Parties."

1. The work

This Agreement covers two pieces of work, priced separately so Client can see exactly what each one includes: Part A, the NI Today client dashboard app, for a one-time fee of $297.00; and Part B, online listings and backlinks, for a one-time fee of $500.00. The total is $797.00. There are no monthly or recurring charges under this Agreement.

2. Part A: the NI Today app (included)

  • One screen, built for Client's phone and usable on any computer, added to the phone's home screen like an app and protected by a six-digit passcode of Client's choosing.
  • Today's and this week's calls from Client's GoHighLevel calendars, each labeled Weekly service call or Event call, with the person's name, the time and what the call is about.
  • A short written summary for each call, generated from that person's intake form: who they are, their household, what they are looking for, allergies and sensitivities, and where they came from (website intake, referral or event form), so Client knows what the call is about before it starts.
  • Every client in one list with their stage (intake received, agreement signed, waiting for a chef, chef assigned), their signed agreement and intake form as PDFs (from the Google Drive records automation delivered on October 9, 2026), and the chef assigned to them.
  • Waiting for a chef: Client picks the chef from a dropdown inside the app. The app assigns the chef and sends that chef the menu and the intake, replacing the Google Sheet step. An all-chefs overview shows each chef's current clients.
  • Upcoming events with the details from the event inquiry form, plus notes and a to-do list that stay on the screen.
  • Push notifications to Client's phone for a new intake and a signed agreement (optional, Client's choice).
  • Live data only. Everything shown comes from GoHighLevel, Google Drive and the client list; nothing is re-entered. GoHighLevel, Make, Google Drive, the client sheet and the calendar keep working in the background exactly as they do today.
  • Hosting on Developer's Netlify account at no additional cost, transferable to an account in Client's name on request.
  • Two (2) rounds of adjustments before launch and thirty (30) days of fixes and small changes after launch (adding, moving or renaming what is on the screen).

3. Part B: online listings and backlinks (included)

  • One consistent set of business facts on every listing: name, phone, website, service area (Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix), category Personal Chef, founded 2014, no street address and no hours shown, Client's own photos and logo, and a different description for each site.
  • Listings Developer sets up, claims or corrects: Yelp (claim and complete the listing; Client provides the login), Google Business Profile (service areas and details), Bing Places, Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps and Siri), Thumbtack, ZoomInfo (correct the outdated address and the "cooking classes" and "catering" wording), Nextdoor, a LinkedIn company page, Bark (optional, Client's choice), and a check that Client's Facebook page and Instagram bio link to www.nutritionintuitionaz.com.
  • Backlinks: ready-to-send messages for Client's chefs (website and Instagram mentions of the Meet the Team page), the Iconic Life feature, five local wellness partners and a local press pitch. Developer sends the ones that can go from Developer's side and hands Client the rest to send from her own accounts.
  • A listings report on completion with the link and status of every listing, and a check thirty (30) days later with any corrections.

4. Not included

  • Paid advertising; per-lead fees on Thumbtack or Bark (Client decides whether to turn those on and pays them directly); guarantees of search rankings or of how search engines and AI assistants describe the business.
  • Third-party subscriptions Client already pays for (GoHighLevel, Make, SignWell, QuickBooks, domain, email). The app works alongside them and does not replace them.
  • Ongoing content, maintenance or new features after the 30-day window. These are available on request and quoted per task; there is no monthly fee.

5. Fees and payment

Part A, NI Today app$297.00: $150.00 on signing and $147.00 when the app is live and Client has seen it.
Part B, listings and backlinks$500.00: $250.00 on signing and $250.00 on delivery of the listings report.
Total$797.00, one-time. No monthly or recurring charges.

Developer sends a PayPal invoice for each payment; Zelle or any other method the Parties agree on in writing is also accepted. Work requested outside Sections 2 and 3 is quoted and approved in writing before it starts.

6. Client responsibilities

  • Provide the Yelp login (or add Developer as a manager) and any other login or approval a listing site requires from the business owner, and decide on the Thumbtack and Bark lead options.
  • Confirm the business facts in Section 3 and the names, rates and details the app shows.
  • Review each round and give feedback within five (5) business days so the work keeps moving.
  • Confirm that Client owns or has permission to use all photos, logos, testimonials and text supplied to Developer.

7. Timeline

Part A: a preview of the app within seven (7) days of the Part A deposit, and launch after Client's approval and the final payment. Part B: listings submitted within fourteen (14) days of receiving the logins and decisions in Section 6; approval and publication by each directory (Yelp, Apple, Bing and the others) happens on their own schedule and is outside Developer's control. Delays in feedback or access extend the timeline by the same number of days.

8. Ownership and credit

On receipt of full payment, Client owns the app's design, its Client-specific content and all data it displays, and every listing is in Client's name. Developer keeps the right to reuse general-purpose code, components and techniques, and may show the app in Developer's portfolio without any of Client's client data. Client's logo, photos and brand remain Client's property at all times.

9. Accounts, access and data

Developer uses Client's GoHighLevel, Google Drive, Make and listing-site access only for the work in this Agreement, does not store Client passwords beyond what the work requires, and hands back or deletes access on request. The app shows Client's client information only behind Client's passcode and is not shared with anyone else. Client remains the owner of all business accounts.

10. Warranty and limitation

Developer warrants that the app will work as described in Section 2 at launch and will fix defects reported within the 30-day window at no charge. Beyond that, the app is provided as-is. Developer is not responsible for changes or outages in third-party services (Netlify, GoHighLevel, Make, Google, SignWell, Yelp or other directories) or for the results of search engines and AI assistants. Developer's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount Client has paid.

11. Termination

Either Party may end this Agreement in writing before the work is complete. If Client ends it, the deposits cover work completed to date and no further payment is due. If Developer ends it for reasons other than Client's breach, Developer refunds any portion of the deposits not yet earned. Work completed and paid for remains Client's.

12. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona. It is the entire agreement between the Parties about the work in Sections 2 and 3 and replaces prior discussions about it; the Website Design and Development Agreement of September 12, 2026 continues to apply to the website. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both Parties. Electronic signatures and copies are accepted.
