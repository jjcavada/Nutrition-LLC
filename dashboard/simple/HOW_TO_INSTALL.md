# NI Today: put it on your phone (for Amber)

Link: https://ni-today.netlify.app

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the link in Safari (not inside Instagram or Gmail, use the real Safari app).
2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
3. Open it from the home screen like any app. Enter the 6 digit code Jay gave you.
4. When it asks about notifications, tap "Allow" so you hear about new intakes and signed agreements. (On iPhone this prompt only appears when the app was opened from the home screen icon.)

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3. Open it from the home screen, enter the code, allow notifications.

**What you see**
- New: people who filled out the intake form and have not signed yet.
- The number boxes open the full lists (clients, waiting for a chef, calls this week).
- Today's calls: your planning calls from your GHL calendars, with a short briefing of what the client wrote, so you can read it before you dial.
- Events: event inquiries from the website.
- Waiting for a chef: pick a chef from the dropdown and confirm. The chef gets the menu and intake form by email, and GHL is updated for you. If there is no menu yet, the app tells you.
- Tap any client card: briefing, allergies, household, phone, Call and Text buttons, and links to their intake PDF and signed agreement in your Google Drive.
- To-do list: your own notes, they stay on your phone.

**Good to know**
- The code locks the app. Tap "Lock" at the bottom if you hand your phone to someone. Ask Jay to change the code any time.
- The app refreshes itself every couple of minutes; pull down or tap Refresh for an instant update.
- Nothing here can delete or change a client. The only thing it writes is the chef you pick.
