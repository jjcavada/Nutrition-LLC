1. The short version

It runs on its own. You steer.

When a new client fills in the intake on your website, they get your welcome email, their agreement to sign, a folder in your Google Drive and a card in NI Today, without you lifting a finger. When they sign, QuickBooks, the chef briefing, the 15-item menu and the consultation invite all happen on their own too.

Your part comes down to four jobs:

  • 1Pick the chef in NI Today after the consultation. The chef gets the intake and the menu by email.
  • 2Approve the chefs' hours in QuickBooks Time by Sunday 6 pm.
  • 3Monday after 8 am, send the invoices. The bot builds them, you check anything flagged and send them from QuickBooks. About 10 minutes.
  • 4Glance at NI Today. Anything under Needs attention is something to look at.

One place for everything: NI Today, the app on your phone's home screen (ni-today.netlify.app). If something looks off, message JJ. Every error also emails JJ automatically, so most of the time he already knows.

3. A client, start to finish

The client journey

Automatic happens on its own. You needs you.

  1. 1

    They fill in the intake Automatic

    On your website (Weekly Service Intake). Then, on its own:

    • A contact and a card in GoHighLevel, with the whole intake saved as a note.
    • Your welcome email with your video.
    • The service agreement sent through SignWell, plus an email with the signing link.
    • A folder in your Google Drive with the intake as a PDF, and a row on your client list.
    • A short AI summary for your call, and a notification on your phone.
  2. 2

    They sign Automatic

    The card moves to Agreement signed, the QuickBooks customer is created, and the signed contract PDF goes into their Drive folder.

  3. 3

    The menu is written Automatic

    The AI writes the chef briefing and a personalized 15-item menu. It sits on the client's profile in the app (it no longer clutters your email). The briefing lists their allergies and a bold red Dislikes (never use) line, and the menu never uses those foods.

  4. 4

    They book the consultation Automatic

    They get the consultation invite with your booking link. When they book, your phone gets a notification.

  5. 5

    You pick the chef You

    In NI Today, under Waiting for a chef. The chef gets one email with the intake and the menu, and the choice is saved in GoHighLevel. Picked the wrong chef? Use Change chef on their profile.

  6. 6

    Weekly service and invoices You

    Chefs clock in on the Workforce app with the receipt photo and the grocery total in the note. You approve the hours; the invoices build themselves Monday morning.

Not continuing? Use Archive client on their profile. Nothing is sent to them, and Put back in the client journey undoes it.

4. NI Today, your app

Everything on one screen

It lives on your home screen like any app and opens with your 6-digit passcode. Lock wipes the client details from the phone until you unlock again. JJ can change the passcode any time.

The home screen

  • Needs attention: anything that went wrong or needs a decision, with what to do.
  • Client journey: how many people are at each step. Tap a step to see who.
  • Calls this week: weekly service and event calls, by day.
  • Event requests: every event inquiry with its date, guests and status.
  • Invoices: this week's invoices, the total and the ones that need a look.
  • See the last 12 months: new leads and new clients by month.

The bell

New intakes, signed agreements, booked consultations and sent invites, with a phone notification for each. Keep the Phone notifications switch inside the bell On, or your phone will not ring for them.

A client's profile

  • What to expect on the call, and a line in their own words.
  • Household, Allergies, and right under it Dislikes: one bold line of everything that must never be used.
  • Didn't pick: the veggies on the form they did not tick, so you and the chef can double check. Not ticking one does not always mean they dislike it.
  • Diet, what they want, area, status, consultation and chef.
  • What the intake form says (the exact allergy and dislike lines), and the notes you type in GoHighLevel.
  • Their intake PDF, signed contract and Drive folder, the full intake, and the chef briefing and menu.
  • Change chef and Archive client.

Waiting for a chef

Three tabs: Waiting (clients who need a chef, longest wait first), Chefs (who has how many clients) and Checklist (each new chef's onboarding, see section 6).

5. Weekly invoicing

Monday invoices, built for you

Live from Monday, October 26. Every Monday at 8 am the bot reads last week's approved hours in QuickBooks and builds every client's invoice the way you do it by hand. It never sends anything. You stay in control of what reaches a client.

The rules it follows (your own)

  • One invoice per client per week, all visits combined, dated Monday, the visit dates in the memo.
  • Hours rounded to the nearest half hour per visit: whole hours as Chef Hours, the half as a Half Hour line.
  • Groceries taken from the amount in the chef's note.
  • One Shopping/Menu Fee line, one per visit day.
  • The 3% card fee for the clients on your card list.
  • Your special rates (for example Tracy Thomas and Rachel Troyan), Due on receipt, the next invoice number.

Flags it raises for you

A note with no grocery amount or several amounts, a very long or very short visit, a busy week, or a client missing from the rate list. Each one shows on the Invoices card with the reason and the day.

Monday, step by step

  1. Open NI Today. The Invoices card shows how many were made, the total and what needs a look.
  2. Tap Open invoices in QuickBooks and fix the flagged ones (usually a grocery amount).
  3. In QuickBooks: Sales, Invoices, select all, Batch actions, Send invoices.

If the hours were not approved by Monday 8 am, nothing is built and the app shows "Invoices not built yet". Approve the hours and tell JJ, he runs it again.

Tested on your real October week before going live: 37 of 43 invoices matched yours to the cent, and the rest were either flagged for you or small differences in the originals. Explainer video: NI invoicing explainer (sent earlier).

6. Chefs

Bringing on a chef

On your website, open Bring on a New Chef (nutritionintuitionaz.com/new-chef) and enter their name, email and phone. Then, on its own:

  • They are added to GoHighLevel and to QuickBooks as a vendor, and to every chef list (the app, GoHighLevel, the assignment sheet).
  • They get the contractor agreement to sign in SignWell.
  • They get the welcome email with the contractor packet, the W-9 and the website profile form for your Meet the Team page.
  • The background check starts in Checkr.
  • When they sign the agreement, your finance team gets their details to add them in Gusto.

The chef checklist

In the app, Waiting for a chef, Checklist tab: 16 steps per chef in five groups. Most tick themselves; tick the rest when you see them done.

Signed up

Form received, name added to every chef list.

Sent to the chef

Welcome email, contractor agreement, background check, W-9, website profile form, QuickBooks Workforce invite.

Back from the chef

Agreement signed, background check cleared, W-9 received, website profile received.

Pay and records

Added in QuickBooks as a 1099 vendor, details sent to finance for Gusto.

Live

Profile live on the website, first client assigned.

Assigning and changing

Pick a chef under Waiting for a chef; the chef gets one email. Change chef on a client's profile switches it and can send the new chef the intake and menu, or just record it. The old chef is not told automatically, so send them a quick text. You are on the chef list yourself.

7. Private events

Event requests

  • When someone uses Plan an Event on your website, you get an email with every detail and a planning call time, and an event agreement draft is ready in SignWell. They get a confirmation email.
  • The Event requests card in the app shows each event with its date, guests, budget, dietary notes, planning call and agreement status.
  • If an event is a week away with no signed agreement and no call, it shows under Needs attention.
  • Chef guide: paste the link to your Google Doc prep guide on the event, and Open chef guide opens it from the app.
  • When the event agreement is signed, they get your booking link for the event consultation.

Event invoices stay manual.

11. What it cannot do yet

And what to do instead

No clock in

If a chef did not clock in for a client, there are no hours, so no invoice. Type that one by hand, or have the chef clock in.

Receipts

Receipt photos are not read. The grocery total has to be written in the chef's note.

Event invoices

Still by hand.

Older clients

Clients from before October 24 never saw the new question. Their Dislikes line comes from what they wrote elsewhere on the form.

Late approvals

Hours approved after Monday 8 am are not picked up on their own. Tell JJ and he runs it again.

Sending

Invoices are never sent by the bot today. Auto send for clean weeks is the next step, only with your OK.

12. If something looks wrong

What to do

  • Check Needs attention in the app first. It says what happened and who it is about.
  • Message JJ. Every automation that fails also emails JJ the step and the client, so he usually knows already.
  • A wrong invoice batch never reaches a client, because nothing is sent until you send it. JJ can delete and rebuild the week.
  • Please do not delete cards in GoHighLevel. Use Archive client in the app instead, so the history stays and you can put them back.
  • Please do not rename SignWell fields or the custom fields in GoHighLevel. The automations depend on the exact names.
  • Testing is always done on test records named ZZTEST or JJ Invoice Test. You can ignore anything with those names.
